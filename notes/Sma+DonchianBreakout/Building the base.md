# Building the base

## Hypothesis

> 20-Period Donchian channel with SMA 50 for broader trend identification and Supertrend as stop-loss

## Rules

### Long Side
>
> - Price Above SMA
> - Price Breaks Above Donchian Channel
> - Price Above SuperTrend
>
> Enter a Long position
>
> Stop-Loss:- 
>    1.5 * ATR
>
> Target:- 
>    2 * ATR


### Short Side
>
> - Price Below SMA
> - Price Breaks Below Donchian Channel
> - Price Below SuperTrend
>
> Enter a Short position
>
> Stop-Loss:- 
>    1.5 * ATR
>
> Target:- 
>    2 * ATR
